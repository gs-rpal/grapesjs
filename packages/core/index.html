Pilot team testing
Your pilot team has been emailed, provisioned, and assembled! Now you'll want to start putting your training materails to good use and jumpstart testing.
Download our sample project plan
While every organization is different, below is a high-level overview of how testing can go:

Pilot team tasks
  • Test the top sites and applications used by your employees to confirm they are functioning seamlessly.
  • Enable and test any SAML apps you intend to use in full roll-out.
  • Test all policies for proper configuration.
  • Test any equivalent domains, allowlists, or blocklists you have enabled for proper set-up and mapping.

Collect feedback from pilot team
  • Account activation and installation process
  • Training experience
  • Overall product experinece

Admin checklist
  • Monitor day-today usage, e.g. adding passwards, asset sharing, and password generation.
  • Survey test group on ease of implementation, training, and satisfaction ratings.
  • Make changes to the implementation strategy as necessary.
